Frequently Asked Questions

Does the AI make up experience I don't have?

Never. Our AI is specifically instructed to only reorganize, rephrase, and optimize your existing experience. It mirrors keywords from the job description and reorders your bullet points for relevance, but it will never fabricate skills, degrees, or accomplishments.

What's an ATS and why does it matter?

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software that companies use to filter resumes before a human sees them. Up to 75% of resumes are rejected by ATS before reaching a recruiter. Our tailoring ensures your resume matches the keywords and phrasing that ATS systems look for.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, absolutely. Cancel anytime from your Account Settings or via the Stripe billing portal. You'll keep access until the end of your current billing period. No questions asked.

Is my resume data safe?

Yes. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell your data, share it with recruiters, or use it for advertising. You can export or delete all your data anytime. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How is this different from ChatGPT?

ResumeTailor is purpose-built for job applications. Our AI prompt is specifically tuned for resume optimization — it knows ATS rules, keyword mirroring, and resume formatting best practices. Plus you get ATS scoring, PDF/DOCX export, cover letters, and your full history saved — all in one focused tool.

What AI model do you use?

We use Anthropic's Claude, one of the most capable and safety-focused AI models available. It excels at following instructions precisely, which is critical for resume tailoring — it won't hallucinate or fabricate experience.
